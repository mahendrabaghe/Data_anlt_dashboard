### Key Observations

- **Electronics** consistently generates the highest revenue among all categories due
  to high unit prices, but its profit margin is compressed by elevated cost-of-goods.
- **Beauty & Personal Care** and **Books** show above-average profit margins despite
  lower absolute revenue, making them attractive categories for growth.
- The **festival months (Oct–Nov)** drive a disproportionate share of annual revenue —
  approximately 2× the monthly average — highlighting the importance of inventory
  planning for Diwali season.

---

## 2. Geographic Performance

- **Maharashtra** and **Delhi** are the top two revenue-generating states, together
  accounting for roughly 30% of total sales.
- **Mumbai** is the highest-revenue individual city, followed by **Bengaluru** and
  **New Delhi**.
- Tier-2 cities (Jaipur, Coimbatore, Lucknow) show growing order volumes, indicating
  an expanding customer base outside metro areas.

---

## 3. Customer Behaviour

- Approximately **60–65%** of customers placed more than one order during the period,
  indicating healthy retention.
- **Repeat customers** generate significantly more revenue per head than new customers,
  reinforcing the business case for loyalty programmes.
- The **26–35 age group** accounts for the largest share of purchases — this segment
  is digitally savvy and responds well to targeted offers.
- **UPI** is the dominant payment method, confirming India's shift to digital payments.

---

## 4. Discount Analysis

- Products offered at **discounts ≥ 30%** show noticeably lower profit margins
  compared to products with discounts below 10%.
- Festival-season discounts (Oct–Nov) increase order volume but reduce per-order
  profitability; the volume lift generally compensates, but margin management is critical.

## 6. Order Fulfilment

- Cancellation rate is approximately **12–14%** of all orders.
- Cancelled orders represent a significant revenue leak; improving logistics, accurate
  ETAs, and easy returns can reduce this rate.
- **Cash on Delivery** orders have a slightly higher cancellation rate than digital
  payment modes — consider incentivising prepaid orders.

---

## 7. Product Recommendations

1. **Expand Electronics accessories** (Power Banks, Earbuds) — high margin, high velocity.
2. **Promote Sports & Fitness** to the 18–25 segment — growing health consciousness.
3. **Bundle Home & Kitchen** products during festive sales for higher basket sizes.
4. **Review Books sub-categories** — Programming and Self-Help books show strong margins.

---

## 8. Strategic Recommendations

1. **Invest in retention**: Repeat customers deliver 2–3× the lifetime value of new
   customers; a structured loyalty programme will improve margins.
2. **Optimise discount strategy**: Capping festival discounts at 20% while maintaining
   volume through limited-time flash sales can protect profitability.
3. **Geo-expand to Tier-2 cities**: States like Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh show
   lower penetration but significant untapped potential.
4. **Improve Cash-on-Delivery conversion**: Implement prepayment incentives to reduce
   cancellations and improve working capital.
5. **Personalise by age segment**: The 36–45 group has high purchasing power — premium
   product recommendations and EMI options can unlock higher AOV.
